Supply & Demand · Prion Exposure & Control · Community Morale

// How Supply Works
At the end of each season, total food and water produced is compared to total consumed. The difference is your surplus (stored) or deficit (shortage). Track food and water separately.
Storage caps: Food spoils after 2 seasons without Vault Storage. Water cannot be stored beyond 1 season without a Dug Well or Sand Filtration Tower.
// Base Consumption per Season
Food: 1 unit per person = 100 food/season at 100 population
Water: 0.8 units per person = 80 water/season at 100 population
Scholars consume separately before the general pool: 4 food + 3 water each/season. Each +1 pop adds 1 food and 0.8 water demand permanently.
// Seasonal Modifiers
Winter: +20% food demand. Fishing -50%. Open crop plots yield 0.
Drought: Natural water sources -40%. Crops -40%.
Flood: Crop plots destroyed unless Greenhouse or Irrigation built. Water contamination +30%.
Post-raid: Up to 50% unprotected stores looted. Vault protects entirely.
// The Grain Mill Effect
The Grain Mill's +25% caloric efficiency means your population extracts more nutrition from the same food. In practice: effective food demand drops from 100 to ~80/season for 100 people.
This is the highest-impact early building for food security. Build it before expanding population.

// What Prion Disease Is
A mutated prion strain released by the collapse. Cannot be killed by boiling, burning, or antibiotics. Destroyed only by the Neutralisation Compound (T4) or prevented by the Vaccine (T5).
Spreads via: contaminated water, untested animals, infected returnees, wasteland proximity. Not contagious person-to-person unless a Symptomatic individual is unquarantined.
// The Exposure Roll
When a trigger event occurs, roll D20. Result at or below the threshold = exposure triggered.
Building threshold reductions:
Detection Lab: -5
Quarantine Suite: -8
Vaccine (vaccinated pop): -10
Prion Barrier: -4 (external sources)
// Individual vs Outbreak
Individual: A named person is exposed. Track them through the stage track. They may recover or spread to others if uncontained.
Settlement outbreak: Uncontained exposure reaches general population. Tracked as a population event with immediate pop and morale consequences.
// Prion vs Physical Contamination
Sand Filtration and boiling handle physical contamination only. Prion requires the Neutralisation Compound - nothing simpler works.
The Detection Lab identifies which type you are dealing with. Without it you cannot distinguish prion from dirty water and cannot begin correct treatment.
Individual Exposure - Stage Track

Each stage lasts 1 season without intervention. Progression is automatic unless treatment is applied. Track each exposed person by name.

Clear
Baseline state
No prion present. Free settlement movement. Detection Lab check on return: D20 at 12 or above confirms clear.
Exposed
Detectable if tested immediately
Traces present, not yet systemic. Quarantine Suite: 90% full clearance after 1 season. Without Quarantine: progresses to Infected. No work penalty this season.
Infected
1 season after Exposed, untreated
Active replication. -50% work effectiveness. Any animal or water they contact: 30% chance of new exposure source. Scholar drops to Weakened condition. Quarantine: 40% clearance chance.
Symptomatic
1 season after Infected, untreated
Cannot work or fight. If unquarantined: D6 roll per close contact each season - on 1 or 2 they become Exposed. Quarantine: 15% clearance only. Can be stabilised but rarely cured now.
Terminal
1 season after Symptomatic
Fatal within the season. Remove from population. If Symptomatic and unquarantined: D6 per close contact - on 1 they become Exposed. Scholar: full inheritance rules apply.

☣ Ground Zero — City Ruins Prion Rules

The city ruins at the map centre carry the original aggressive prion mutation. Standard threshold reductions still apply, but all base thresholds at Ground Zero are +4 higher than their normal values regardless of what buildings you have.

City strain wound rule: Any fighter wounded at Ground Zero rolls D6. On a 1: permanently exposed to the city strain — their personal prion threshold is +2 higher for all future checks, even after recovery. This is irreversible without the Vaccine Prototype.

City strain prion samples: Samples collected at Ground Zero count as 2 Research Points each in the Prion Research Laboratory rather than 1. They are the fastest path to the Vaccine but the hardest to obtain safely.

Mandatory prerequisites for Ground Zero expeditions: Prion Detection Lab + Quarantine Suite are not optional here — without both, all returning members are automatically Exposed on return regardless of roll result.
Most Common Mistake: Players delay the Detection Lab because it produces nothing visible. By the time an outbreak starts it is too late. Without the Lab you cannot confirm exposure, and without confirmation you cannot begin quarantine. Build the Detection Lab as your first T3 priority - before irrigation, before the Blast Furnace, before anything else. One undetected infected animal can cascade into a Major Outbreak killing 20+ people before you can respond.
// The Morale Score
Your settlement has a single Morale Score tracked from −10 to +10. It starts at 0 (Stable) at game start. It moves up and down based on events each season. Check the current score at the start of each season and apply its effects before any other actions.
// How It Moves
Morale changes by the sum of all modifier events that occurred last season. Multiple events stack. There is no roll — modifiers are applied directly from the table. Score is capped at +10 and −10.
Morale recovers naturally by +1 per season when food supply is Adequate or above and no negative events occurred.
// When It Matters
Morale affects worker efficiency, militia combat rolls, Scholar satisfaction, and whether population growth occurs. High morale accelerates everything. Broken morale is a genuine crisis — workers slow down, fighters lose nerve, and people start leaving.
// Morale vs. Prion
The two systems interact. A prion outbreak in the settlement is one of the largest morale hits in the game. Conversely, low morale increases the chance that people cut corners on prion protocols — at Poor or Broken, workers returning from expeditions have a 15% chance of skipping the Detection Lab check.

Recovery & Crisis Rules
// Natural Recovery
If no negative events occurred last season and food supply is Adequate or better: morale recovers +1 automatically at the start of the next season before any other modifiers are applied. This means sustained stability gradually pulls the score upward without any specific action required.
// Breaking Out of Poor / Broken
Natural recovery alone is not enough to climb out of Poor or Broken morale. The settlement needs a deliberate positive event — a major building, a decisive raid win, a successful alliance, or an archive discovery. Without one, the score stagnates at the floor.
// Desertion at Poor / Broken
At Poor morale: roll D6 at season end. On a 1, D3 workers leave permanently. At Broken: roll D6 on a 1–2, D6 workers leave. Scholars at Broken morale trigger their normal defection check at −5 to their satisfaction score on top of any existing modifiers.
// Morale & The End Game
Launching the Ocean Ark Fleet requires morale to be at Neutral or above. A community that has broken down cannot mount a coordinated ocean voyage. If morale is Poor or Broken when the Fleet is otherwise ready, the launch is delayed until recovery — which can itself become a race against the Remnant Authority.
⚠ Most Common Mistake: Ignoring morale until it becomes a crisis. The −1 modifiers from Rationing and slow seasons accumulate quietly over many seasons, and by the time a raid happens on top of them, the score drops into Broken territory faster than any single positive event can counteract. Check the score every season and act early.
