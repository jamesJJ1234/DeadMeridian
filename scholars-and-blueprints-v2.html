// What is a Scholar?
Scholars are named individual characters — the rarest human resource in the game. They are the only people who retain deep pre-collapse technical knowledge. Without them, Tier III blueprints and above are unreadable. Each Scholar has: Domain, Knowledge Tier (K1–K3), Condition, and Satisfaction (−20 to +20).
// Upkeep Costs (per Scholar/season)
Food: 4 units/season — drawn from settlement food pool
Water: 3 units/season — drawn from clean water supply
Rationing: −2 Sat/season · Starvation: −5 Sat/season · Water shortage: −3 Sat/season
// Scholars vs Worker Pool
Scholars are not drawn from the 80-worker pool. They are tracked independently as named characters. Their upkeep (food + water) is still deducted from the settlement supply each season.
A settlement can theoretically hold as many Scholars as it can feed, but each one above 3 competes for blueprint-relevant work.
// Dual-Domain Scholars
Cannot be trained — only discovered or recruited. Count as two Scholars for blueprint interpretation.
Upkeep: +50% · Max Satisfaction: +16 (reduced ceiling due to cognitive burden)
Acquisition Routes
🔍 Discovery
Found during an expedition — an encounter event. Situation must be resolved (negotiation, rescue, or persuasion). Starting Sat: +2 to +8 depending on circumstances.
🤝 Recruitment
Hired through NPC faction diplomacy or trade. Sat on arrival: +2 to +6. May come with demands — always specify working conditions in negotiation.
🎓 Training
Apprenticeship Programme (T3 building). 3–4 seasons. Trained Scholar starts at K1, Sat +5. Partial Scholar (interrupted) starts K1, Sat +3. Requires an existing Scholar to teach.
Knowledge Domains
⚙ Engineering

Structures, water systems, mechanical devices, vessel construction. Most broadly useful domain. Almost every mid and late-game build touches Engineering.

🌱 Agriculture

Crop systems, livestock, irrigation, food preservation. Without one, food output plateaus at Tier II permanently.

🩺 Medicine

Surgery, disease treatment, prion management. Most directly responsible for keeping your other Scholars alive.

🧪 Chemistry

Purification, compounds, prion neutralisation and vaccine chain. Pairs with Medicine on most critical late-game technologies.

⚒ Metallurgy

Forging, steel production, armour, advanced tools. Less urgent early but gates the entire military and structural branch by mid-game.

⚡ Electronics

All electrical systems. Dormant until Tier IV generator unlocks, then instantly one of the rarest and most sought-after domains on the map.

K1→K2: requires 3 successful builds overseen. K2→K3: requires 6 successful builds + at least 1 completed apprentice. Failed or abandoned builds do not count.

// What is a Blueprint?
Physical documents — schematics, manuals, annotated notes. They exist as objects that must be physically found, carried, and protected. No blueprint = no technology. They can be stolen, burned, or water-damaged permanently.
// D20 Discovery Roll
Roll D20 on a dedicated blueprint search expedition to a specific site. Blueprints are never found incidentally during normal scavenging. Apply all bonuses before comparing to the required roll.
Scholar on Field Assignment: +4 flat to ALL blueprint rolls, regardless of tier or domain.
// Interpretation Requirements
Finding a blueprint opens a construction chain. The blueprint must be interpreted before building can begin.
T1–T2: Basic literacy (no Scholar) · T3: Scholar required · T4: Scholar + domain expertise · T5: Often 2+ Scholars required
// Blueprint Damage & Loss
Fire: Permanent if unprotected. Raiders may burn deliberately.
Theft: Undetected until you attempt to use it.
Water damage: Degrades in poor storage.
Document Printing Press (T4): Creates backup copies — eliminates loss risk from fire or theft.
