🎓 Scholar Defection Protocols
Triggers · Procedure · Consequences · Recovery
Scholars & Blueprints — Scholar Tab
Defection triggers during Phase 4 (Population & Morale) when a Scholar's Satisfaction reaches −13 or lower at the end of Satisfaction resolution (Step 20). At −13 they enter Breaking Point — roll each season. At −20 they defect automatically with no roll. Defection is declared by the GM and takes effect immediately before player actions begin.
Breaking Point Roll (−13 to −19)
Roll D20 at the end of each season the Scholar is at Breaking Point.
1–6 — Defects this season. Proceed to defection procedure below.
7–13 — Stays but visibly unstable. Refuses one assignment next season (GM chooses which).
14–19 — Holds on. No immediate action but Sat cannot improve above −10 until a major gesture is made.
20 — Committed despite everything. Sat resets to −8. They have found a reason to stay — roleplay the reason.
Automatic Defection (−20)
No roll — Scholar leaves immediately at the end of the season.
They take any personal blueprints they were assigned to (items they physically held). Shared settlement blueprints remain — they cannot carry what is locked in storage.
If Vault Storage is built: all blueprints are secured. Scholar departs empty-handed on knowledge items.
Remaining Scholars each take −3 Sat immediately (witnessing a colleague leave is demoralising regardless of reason).
Where They Go — D6 Destination Roll
D6DestinationImmediate EffectLong-Term Consequence
1 Joins The Reapers Reapers gain access to Scholar's domain knowledge. Their weapon or build tier may improve within 2 seasons. Reapers now know your settlement's blueprint layout. Raids become more targeted — they go for specific resources.
2 Joins The Covenant Covenant relationship improves one step (they value the Scholar). Scholar is treated well — high Sat within 1 season. Covenant may use Scholar's knowledge as leverage in trade negotiations. Expect inflated prices on Scholar-relevant goods.
3 Joins The Drowned The Drowned gain your Scholar's domain. An Engineering or Electronics Scholar is especially dangerous here — they may improve their vessel or electronics capability. The Drowned start denying you access to submerged zones unless you pay a sharply increased toll.
4 Goes Solo — Wasteland Scholar disappears into wasteland. No faction gains their knowledge immediately. After 1D3 seasons, they either: die (50%), are found by The Silent and become a bargaining chip (30%), or return willing to negotiate re-hire (20%) at significant cost.
5 Joins Remnant Authority Remnant Authority gains Scholar. This is the most dangerous outcome — they already have the most resources and this strengthens their position further. Remnant Authority may use Scholar knowledge to identify your weaknesses and apply targeted diplomatic pressure or increase tribute demands.
6 Stays Local — Independent Scholar leaves your settlement but does not join a faction. Sets up independently within 20–40 blocks of your bunker. Can be re-recruited at a cost (see Recovery below). If left uncontacted for 2+ seasons, roll D6: on 1–3 they are absorbed by a random faction.
Recovery — Getting Them Back
SituationRecovery MethodCostRoll Required
Independent (D6 result 6) Send a diplomatic envoy. Offer improved conditions — address whatever caused the Sat collapse (food, domain work, safety). 30 food + 10 medicine + a guaranteed domain-relevant build within 1 season. D20 + Diplomacy Mod. 12+: returns at Sat +2. Below 12: refuses. Retry next season at −1 cumulative.
Joined a Neutral/Trading faction Negotiate Scholar return as a trade item. Faction will not give them up cheaply — they see the Scholar as an asset. 1 Tier III Blueprint + 20 steel + 20 food minimum. Faction may demand more based on Scholar's K-tier. D20 + Diplomacy Mod at Trading or better. 14+: faction releases Scholar. Scholar returns at Sat 0.
Joined a Hostile faction Cannot negotiate directly. Options: military retrieval (raid to extract), wait for faction relationship to improve, or accept the loss and train a replacement. Military retrieval: combat event. Scholar must survive the extraction. D6: 1–2 severely injured in the attempt. No diplomacy option while Hostile. Combat retrieval: standard raid rules apply.
Gone Solo — Wasteland Send scouts to search the wasteland. Expedition to locate them. Scout expedition (Zone 2–3). Uses 1 expedition slot. Prion checks on return. D20 search roll, Tricky (10+). On success: found and can be offered re-hire at the Independent cost above.
Scholar is K3 (Master) All recovery costs doubled. Factions holding a K3 Scholar know exactly what they have. Effectively: 2× all resource costs listed above. All recovery rolls at −3. A K3 Scholar who defected has likely found someone who treats them better — make it personal.
Prevention is always cheaper than recovery. The most common Sat collapse causes are: food rationing (−2/season), wrong domain assigned (−2/season), and water shortage (−3/season) — all of which compound silently over 3–4 seasons before hitting Breaking Point. Check Scholar Sat every Phase 4 without skipping it. A Scholar sitting at −8 for two seasons is a crisis waiting to happen.
Ground Zero Expedition — Special Season Rules
Prerequisites before sending: Prion Detection Lab built · Quarantine Suite built · Scout Training Grounds built · Watchtower Network built · Minimum 10 expedition members · Quarantine protocol assigned for return.

Travel cost: 2 full seasons minimum (1 there, 1 back). Expedition is offline for the full duration — cannot be recalled.

On return: All members undergo mandatory Quarantine Suite processing before re-entering the settlement. This takes 0.5 seasons. Even with Quarantine Suite, run prion checks at the Ground Zero threshold (+4 to all base thresholds) for every member.

Faction awareness: Any expedition to Ground Zero triggers the Reapers awareness check (D6: 1–3 = patrol sent next season). After 3 player expeditions, the Remnant Authority also becomes aware and begins their own archive push.

Archive access: Reaching the Government Quarter requires 3 separate expeditions minimum — first to locate, second to assess, third to extract. A Medicine Scholar present on all three reduces this to 2. The Silent can reduce it to 1 if relationship is Friendly or above.
