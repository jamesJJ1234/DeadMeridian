Dead Meridian Combat Reference Player Edition

Combat

Raids · Encounters · Weapons · Fortifications · Casualties

// The Attack Roll
Roll D20 + Attack Value + Weapon Bonus + Modifiers. Subtract the defender's Defence Value. The result is the margin — which band it falls in determines the outcome for that round.
// Combat Scale
Combat resolves at group level. Each side rolls once per group per round. Individual weapon stats are averaged across the group. A group can be any size from 2 to your full militia.
// When Combat Triggers
Raid: A faction attacks your settlement.
Expedition Encounter: Scouts meet hostiles in the field.
Assault: You attack another faction.
Skirmish: Fewer than 6 fighters per side.
// Time Cost
Raid defence: 0 seasons. Assault on a defended position: 0.5 seasons. Siege: 1+ seasons. Fighters in combat roles cannot produce resources that season.

Nat 1: Always a Miss. Attacker suffers 1 Injured from friendly fire.    Nat 20: Always a Critical. May name one specific individual as the casualty.
Round Sequence
1
Declare
Both sides secretly choose Attack, Hold, or Withdraw. Reveal simultaneously. Cannot change once revealed.
2
Initiative
Both roll D20. Higher acts first. Tie: defender first. Scouts +3 in field encounters. Ambush: attacker goes first automatically.
3
Ranged
LR bolt-action first, then rifles and SMGs, then pistols and bows. Apply casualties before melee resolves.
4
Melee
Both sides roll simultaneously. Hold groups add +2 DEF to all allies at same position and absorb the first allied casualty each round.
5
Morale
Any side taking 30%+ casualties this round rolls a Morale Check (D20 + modifier). Failure: group Withdraws next round involuntarily.
6
Repeat
Continue until one side Withdraws, surrenders, or is eliminated. Max 6 rounds before mutual exhaustion — treat as a draw.
Morale
// Morale Check Triggers
30%+ casualties in one round · Leader killed · Settlement breached · 50%+ total casualties in the engagement.
// Morale Roll
D20 + modifier. 10+: hold position. 7–9: fall back one position, lose Hold bonus. 1–6: full Withdraw.
Modifiers: Militia +3 · Scouts +2 · Conscripts −2 · Palisade Wall present +2 · Leader alive +2 · Outnumbered 2:1+ −3
// Withdrawal
Withdrawing groups flee. 25% chance of 1 additional casualty during flight. The opposing side may pursue with Scouts — on Hit or better deal +D6 casualties to the retreating group.
// Surrender
Either side may surrender at the start of any round. No further casualties. Prisoners cost 1 food + 1 water per season. Options: ransom to their faction, recruit, or release.
Unit Types
⚔ Militia
ATK +3 DEF +4 Morale +3
Trained defenders. Full combat effectiveness in all roles. Starting allocation: 12. Expand to 18 for high-raid environments. Can hold positions, flank, and pursue. Return to full readiness after 1 season off combat duty.
🗺 Scouts
ATK +2 DEF +2 Initiative +3
Mobile and fast. +3 to initiative in field encounters. Best for ambush, flanking, retreat, and pursuit. Cannot hold fortified positions without −2 DEF penalty. Use to outmanoeuvre, not to man walls.
⚙ Conscripted Workers
ATK +0 DEF +1 Morale −2
Untrained fighters. Terrible in open combat. Only useful behind a fortification bonus manning walls. If over 50% of your force are conscripts, all morale rolls take −3. Never send on expeditions in combat roles.
⚠ Scholar Safety: Scholars present in the settlement during a raid are not automatically in combat. If the settlement is breached, roll D6 per Scholar: on a 1 they are caught in the fighting and roll on the Injury track. A Masterwork long-range bolt-action can target a named Scholar before combat begins — Nat 20 required.
// Weapon Assignment
Weapons are assigned to groups, not individuals. A group's weapon stat is the most common weapon type in that group. Mixed groups use the lower bonus.
Example: 8 militia — 5 Standard pistols, 3 Crude → group uses Standard stats.
// Range Bands
Close: pistols, melee, SMGs, shortbow.
Medium: bolt-actions, shortbow, longbow.
Full: semi-auto rifles, longbow.
Extreme: LR bolt-action only.
Firing outside range: −4 to roll.
// Jam Rules
With scavenged ammo: D10 per group each round. On a 1, one weapon jams — that fighter cannot attack this round. Crude weapons jam on 1–2. Clearing costs the fighter's next full action. Bullet Press ammo: no jams.
// Ammo Per Round
Pistol: 2 small · SMG: 6 small · Bolt: 1 large · Semi-auto: 3 large · LR bolt: 1 large · Bow: 3 arrows.

Track per group. Group that runs out of ammo/arrows drops to melee for remaining rounds.

Bows — Crafted Weapons
No ammo cost. No jam. Ever. Bows consume arrows (3 per group per round) instead of bullets. Arrows are crafted at the Carpentry School from Timber only — no Gun Bench, no bullet components. The trade-off is lower ATK than equivalent firearms and no suppression or Extreme range options.

// Defence Value
Every defending group has a base DEF from unit type plus fortification bonuses. Attackers must beat the total with their margin to inflict casualties.

Militia: DEF +4 · Scouts: DEF +2 · Conscripts: DEF +1
// Perimeter Layers
Your settlement has an Outer and an Inner perimeter. Raiders must breach the outer before engaging defenders directly. A Critical result against a perimeter position breaches it — cannot be repaired mid-combat.
// Hold Formation
Groups assigned to Hold do not attack that round but add +2 DEF to all allies at their position and absorb the first casualty inflicted on those allies each round.
// Night Raids
Night raids: all attack rolls −2 for both sides. The Watchtower Network completely negates this penalty for your defenders. Without it, your fighters suffer the same penalty as the attackers.
Fortification Buildings
Palisade Wall (Tier I)
+4 DEF for all defenders on the outer perimeter. −20% raid damage to structures if the perimeter holds for the full engagement.
Watchtower Network (Tier II)
+2 warning seasons before any raid. Negates night raid penalty completely. +2 initiative on all raid encounters.
Scout Training Grounds (Tier III)
Scouts gain +1 ATK and +1 DEF. Allows Flanking: one Scout group removes the defender's fortification bonus for one round.
Reinforced Bunker Extension (Tier IV)
+6 DEF on the inner perimeter. Requires 2 Critical hits to breach. Defenders inside gain +2 morale.
Prion Barrier / Burn Wall (Tier III)
Attackers crossing the Burn Wall roll D6: on 1–2 take 1 automatic Injury before engaging. +2 DEF as additional outer layer.
Reinforced Vault Storage (Tier IV)
All resources stored inside are immune to raider looting even if the settlement is fully breached.

// Casualties Are Permanent
Every fighter lost is a worker, farmer, or defender gone. Combat is meant to feel costly. Winning a raid with 8 dead is not a clean victory — it is a crisis that will take seasons to recover from.
// Casualty Tracks
After combat, all casualties sort into: Injured (recover), Severely Injured (may die without treatment), and Killed (permanent). Deduct Killed from population immediately.
// Medical Buildings
None: Severely Injured — 50% death chance per season without treatment.
Field Surgery Station (T1): −25% injury-to-death. Treats 5/season.
Advanced Surgery Theatre (T3): −50%. Treats 15/season. Severe cases recover in 0.5 seasons.
// Settlement Morale
After any raid or assault — win or lose — your settlement suffers −2 morale for 2 seasons. If you decisively repelled the attack with fewer than 10% casualties, gain +2 morale instead.
